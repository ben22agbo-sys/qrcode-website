Step 1: Get your Google review link

  1. Open your Google Business Profile. Sign in to the Google account that manages your business, then search for your business name on Google or go to Google Business Profile.
  2. Find the review option. Click Ask for reviews. Google sometimes labels it Get more reviews.
  3. Copy the link. It's a short link that opens your review form. If you manage more than one location, each has its own link, so check you're on the right one.

The example link in the generator above is a placeholder, so swap it for your own before downloading.

Step 2: Make your QR code

  1. Paste your review link into the Link box, replacing the example.
  2. Add your logo and colours so customers can tell it's yours.
  3. Download and test it. Scan it with your phone and check it opens your review form, not someone else's.

Where it works best

Put it where customers are happy and have a moment to spare: on the counter by the till, on receipts and takeaway bags, on a thank-you card in an order, or on a sign by the exit. Add a short, friendly line like "Enjoyed your visit? Leave us a review".

Keep within Google's rules

Google doesn't allow offering discounts or gifts in exchange for reviews, or only asking customers you expect to leave good ones. Ask everyone, and let them say what they think.

Questions

Google review QR code questions.

Do customers need a Google account to leave a review?

Yes. Google asks people to sign in before they post a review. Anyone already signed in to Google on their phone goes straight to the form.

I can't find "Ask for reviews". What now?

Make sure you're signed in with the Google account that manages the business profile, and that the profile has been verified. Without that, the option doesn't appear.

Can I use one code for several locations?

No. Each location has its own review link, so make a separate code for each one. Otherwise reviews end up on the wrong branch.

Is this free?

Yes. The code, your logo, colours and downloads are all free, with no watermark.
