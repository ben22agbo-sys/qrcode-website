First, put your menu online

A QR code holds a link, so your menu needs an address on the web. Any of these work:

  • A menu page on your website, like yourrestaurant.com/menu. This is the best option, because it loads quickly on phones and is easy to read.
  • A PDF stored online, for example in Google Drive or Dropbox, with sharing set so anyone with the link can view it.
  • Your page on a booking or delivery app, if your full menu is already there.
Link to a page you can update

The link is printed into the code, so it can't be changed later. If you link to an address that stays the same, like /menu, you can update prices and dishes on that page and every printed code keeps working. Avoid links to a specific file name, like menu-spring-v2.pdf, that you'll replace next season.

  4. Test it in your restaurant, under your own lighting, with two or three different phones.

Printing tips for tables and windows

  • Size: about 3 to 4 cm wide for a table card scanned at arm's length. A window sign read from a couple of metres away needs to be much bigger. See the print size guide.
  • Add a short instruction next to the code, like "Scan for our menu".
  • Avoid shiny finishes where lights reflect straight back at the camera. Matte lamination works better than gloss.
  • Keep a few paper menus for customers without a smartphone or with low battery.
Questions

Menu QR code questions.

Can I change my menu after printing the QR code?

Yes, as long as the link stays the same. Update the menu page or replace the file at the same address, and the printed codes keep working.

Do customers need an app to scan it?

No. Most phones scan QR codes straight from the camera app.

Will the QR code stop working?

No. The link is stored in the pattern itself, so there's no subscription or account that can expire. It works for as long as your menu link does.

Can I put my restaurant's logo in the middle?

Yes, for free. The QR code with logo page explains how big it can be and still scan.
