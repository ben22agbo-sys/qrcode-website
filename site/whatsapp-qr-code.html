How a WhatsApp QR code works

WhatsApp has an official link format called click to chat. It looks like https://wa.me/447700900123, with your full number at the end. Opening it starts a chat with you, without anyone having to save your number first.

You can add a message too, like ?text=Hi at the end. It appears in the person's message box ready to send, and they can edit it first. Nothing is sent until they tap send.

Writing your number the right way

  • Start with your country code, like 44 for the UK, 1 for the US and Canada, or 234 for Nigeria.
  • Drop the first 0 of your local number. A UK mobile 07700 900123 becomes 447700900123.
  • Leave out the plus sign, spaces, brackets and dashes. The helper above does this for you.

Ideas for using it

  • Bookings and orders: a message like "Hi, I'd like to book a table for" saves customers typing.
  • Shop windows and flyers: people can ask a question even when you're closed.
  • Packaging and receipts: an easy route for customer service or reorders.
Use a number you're happy to share

Your number is visible to anyone who scans the code. If you run a business, WhatsApp Business lets you keep your customer chats separate from your personal ones.

Questions

WhatsApp QR code questions.

Does the message send automatically?

No. It's typed into the chat box for the customer, who can change it and chooses when to send.

What if someone doesn't have WhatsApp?

The link opens a WhatsApp web page instead, which asks them to download the app or use WhatsApp on the web.

Can I change the message after printing?

No. The message is part of the link inside the code, so a different message means a new code.

Does it work with WhatsApp Business?

Yes. The same link format works for WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business numbers.
